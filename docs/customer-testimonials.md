## Content and rights policy

- No made-up testimonials, synthesized customer statements, fabricated star ratings, dates, client names or testimonials inspired by but falsely attributed to real people.
- Never scrape, bulk-copy, cache or rehost Google Maps review content outside Google services. Google's Maps Platform terms restrict this; supported Places review display follows API attribution and link-back policies, and may require billing.
- **Low-friction initial approach:** provide a clear link such as “Read customer reviews on Google” leading to the verified official profile. Consider an approved Google embed/Places integration only after assessing terms, author attribution, source linking, cost and freshness.
- **Curated on-site quotations:** only from an original customer-provided statement or a Google reviewer with appropriate off-platform permission. Keep wording faithful; if excerpting, do not change sentiment or introduce misleading ellipses.
- Identify author in the form they authorised (e.g. full name, first name, initials or “Client, [verified event type]”). Keep consent and event identity records out of the public repository.
- Do not display Google branding, live numeric rating or review counts unless they are correctly sourced and compliant. Never freeze a current metric as permanently true.
- Video: confirm testimonial participants' publication/likeness permissions; filmmaker/music licences; no private guests without consent; captions/transcript; poster image; accessible player; explicit play by default (no autoplay audio). Prefer original, optimised, rights-cleared uploads, with an Instagram source link when relevant.
