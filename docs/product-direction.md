# The Wedding Nest — product and design direction

## Purpose
Generate qualified wedding and event service enquiries in Hyderabad with an exceptional, authentic luxury editorial experience. The owner moderates visual design, product flows and copy in a web browser.

## Audience and scope (verify before publishing)
Wedding and associated celebrations: Nikah, Walima, Mehndi, engagement and other community traditions; services under consideration include planning/coordination, catering, venue/vendor management, floral design, decor, photography/video, makeup, transport, trousseau and gifts. **This is a product scope hypothesis, not a public claim that TWN supplies every service.** Ask the business owner for confirmed service coverage and permissible examples.

## Visual vision
Authentic photography-led, restrained and cinematic, not generic SaaS tiles or AI-looking wedding stock scenes. Premium typography, intentional asymmetry, considered whitespace, elegant editorial hierarchy, sophisticated yet quiet interactions. Motion has a storytelling purpose; honor prefers-reduced-motion, keyboard operation, touch, low-end devices and Core Web Vitals. Never invent a testimonial or portfolio event.

## Core user journeys
- Quickly understand the offer and service coverage.
- Browse selected real work and the range of event experiences.
- Discover service-specific information, trust and straightforward contact.
- Enter a guided, accessible enquiry canvas that adapts by occasion and permits skipping budget.
- Submit only after explicit consent to processing contact details, with server-side confirmation and an accessible failure/retry path.
- Offer a verified WhatsApp contact route only when the business provides its approved number.

## Assets required
Approved logo, original event photography/video with publication rights, verified services and coverage, contact details, hours, legal/privacy text, enquiry handling owners, authentic testimonials with permission. Until supplied, mark the content as placeholder and do not ship production claims.
